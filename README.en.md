<h1 align="center">Where Car</h1>

🚙 **The Smartest Way to Manage Your Vehicles**

From corporate fleets to rental and car-sharing services,  
**manage everything** from real-time tracking to automated trip logs—all in one platform.

We built this platform to support rental companies in managing and monitoring vehicles **more efficiently and systematically**.

It’s easy for users, and powerful for businesses.
